# Landing home (/)

Scope: the home route and the shell it shares (navbar, footer). Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: building administrators and facility managers in Lima deciding whether to book HTL's free diagnostic visit. Proof on hand: brochure photography, owner-supplied metrics, verified software features. Constraint: both themes, three.js hero, brand red #810303.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a building and the visitor rides it. Sections are floors, the navigation is the lobby directory, and a floor indicator tracks the scroll. It refuses the split hero with a stock elevator photo and a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Lobby signage and cabin steel. Carbon and graphite grounds (cool steel white in light theme), brushed-steel plates with square corners, whole floors drenched in #810303, an LED-red seven-segment readout, one variable grotesque set condensed and heavy for lettering and at normal width for reading. Controls are call buttons and directory rows.

STORY: The visitor sees a closed elevator carrying the promise, opens it by scrolling, walks into the red cabin wall, reads the numbers on a capacity plate, checks the services floor by floor, watches a work order travel from schedule to signature, and books the visit on the top floor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A three.js elevator centred in a lobby wall, doors closed with a slit of light. The headline "Su edificio no se detiene" is lettered across both door leaves and splits with them. Desktop: directory board on the left wall, offer line plus the red call-button CTA on the right wall. Mobile: doors fill the upper two thirds, offer and CTA sit under the threshold in thumb reach. The floor readout sits in the top bar.

FORM: A building lobby directory board, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list, fused with the brief-pinned elevator hero. Seed key 3c847436. Raise from the declined metalwork-showroom challenger: every plate, row and control snaps to one square-cornered lattice, no soft cards. Raise from the declined teletext challenger: every floor has a keyed address shown identically in the nav, the readout and the section plate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
